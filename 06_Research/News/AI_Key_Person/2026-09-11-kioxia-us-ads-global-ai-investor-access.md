# Kioxia — U.S. ADS listing may widen global AI-investor access

担当: ❤️レイ
種別: AI Key Person Watch / Research
Date: 2026-09-11
Status: MATERIAL_DELTA

## Observation

Reuters reported on 2026-09-11 that Kioxia Holdings is planning a U.S. listing through American Depositary Shares (ADS) to broaden its investor base.

Sebastian Thomas, portfolio manager at Voya Investment Management, said the U.S. listing could put Kioxia more directly into the global AI-investment spotlight by improving liquidity and accessibility for global investors. Reuters described Voya's Global Artificial Intelligence strategy as a major AI-focused fund and noted that Kioxia is not currently in that fund.

Source:
- Reuters, 2026-09-11: `US listing could put Japan's Kioxia in global AI spotlight, Voya's Thomas says`
  https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/us-listing-could-put-japans-kioxia-global-ai-spotlight-voyas-thomas-says-2026-09-11/

## Inference

This does **not** change Kioxia's earnings outlook by itself and is not evidence of additional NAND orders, revenue or profit.

The investment-relevant change is a potential market-access channel:

```text
AI / memory demand narrative
→ U.S. ADS listing / improved global accessibility
→ broader institutional AI-investor universe
→ liquidity / investor-base expansion
→ possible valuation re-rating
```

This is therefore classified as **market-access / re-rating evidence**, not earnings-conversion evidence.

## Japanese-stock implication

Kioxia already has direct AI-infrastructure exposure through memory demand and large-scale capacity investment. A U.S. ADS listing could reduce an additional friction identified by global AI investors: difficulty accessing/liquidity in Japanese technology equities.

For Sado Investment Lab, the next important check is whether this proceeds from reported plan to a formal filing/listing timetable and whether foreign institutional ownership/liquidity measurably increases afterward.
